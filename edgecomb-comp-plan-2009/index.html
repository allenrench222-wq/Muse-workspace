Town of Edgecomb, Maine · Comprehensive Plan

Edgecomb’s plan, then and now

Key points from the plan’s analysis

  • The Future Land Use Plan kept the existing district map “as adopted in 2003 and projected to be valid for the next fifteen to twenty years” (p. 10) — that window is the one now closing.
  • Public water and sewer serve only the Davis Island TIF district — the plan puts sewer and water at “only 1% of land in Edgecomb” (Part 1, p. 2) and the TIF service area at 0.32% of the town’s area (p. 11).
  • Retail buildings are capped at 35,000 square feet, with design standards to keep buildings over 10,000 square feet at a residential scale (p. 8).
  • The plan named “the major and vital capital improvement need at this time” as a new fire station (p. 11), and warned that administrative capacity “is at present strained” — a volunteer planning board and a part-time codes officer (pp. 8–9).
  • An accessory apartment provision allows a unit inside an existing one-family dwelling without changing the building’s footprint, “providing an opportunity for affordable housing” (pp. 6–8).
